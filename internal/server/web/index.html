Quick start
  1. Tap a look in the row below to play it.
  2. Or tap Auto and play music: the lights follow it by themselves.
  3. Tap Spots, Chauvet wash or Spider to change just that light.
  4. Schedule runs a whole night on its own.

Keys on the Mac: A Auto · B Blackout · F Flash (hold) · S Strobe · 1–9 looks

Moods

A mood is a whole look: the colors, which program is running, its speed (energy) and variation, and the master level. Set the lights how you want them on the Perform tab, then come back and save it here.

Fade when I tap Play
Points & music

Mark a few points and the mood glides from one to the next, round and round. To mark a point: set the lights how you want them (Perform tab, any layout), then tap Mark point. The button also shows on the Perform tab while this is open. Tap a point to see it on the lights.

Music no beat

The music comes from the TAP button or the Mac's mic (the same as music mode on the Perform tab). When no beat has come in for a few seconds, the mood goes back to its own speed and stops pulsing.

Schedule

Each line plays a mood at a time, and it keeps playing until the next line. Untick days to skip them. Times after midnight count as the next day, so 2:00 AM on a Friday night is Saturday. The fade is how long it takes to blend into the new mood.

Fixture presets

A preset is a fixture's set of sliders: one per DMX channel, in order, each with a name and a type. Make one for a light whose sliders are wrong, then apply it to every fixture of that model.

Your presets

Built in (open one to save your own copy)

Pick a preset on the left, or start a new one.

New preset

Channel 1 is the light's start address. Type tells the app what a slider does: pan and tilt drive the aim pad, dimmer follows the master and the Light on/off buttons, and so on; use generic if none fits. Buttons are optional named values shown under the slider, written as Name=value, for example White=0, Red=14, Blue=28 for a color wheel.

#Slider nameTypeStart atButtons

Use it on

Ticked fixtures switch to these sliders when you save. Fixtures already using this preset always update. Their address does not change.

Channel sweep

While a sweep runs it replaces the normal output (everything not listed is 0), so there is only one Art-Net sender. Use Manual to step at your own pace and note what each channel does.
